    About CreditLens

    What CreditLens analyzes

    Every assessment uses the same eleven inputs. They cover the borrower, the structure and pricing of the loan, and the applicant's credit history.

    Borrower profile

    Age, annual income, home ownership status and employment length.

    Loan structure

    Loan intent, loan grade, requested amount and loan-to-income ratio.

    Credit history

    Prior default on file, credit history length and the interest rate offered.

    How it works

    From application to classification in four steps

    1. 01

      Enter applicant data

      Complete the borrower, loan and credit history fields. Loan-to-income is calculated for you.

    2. 02

      Validate inputs

      Every value is checked against its allowed range before anything is sent to the model.

    3. 03

      Score the application

      The application is sent to the prediction service, which returns a default probability.

    4. 04

      Compare to threshold

      Probabilities at or above the decision threshold are classified as High Risk. Others are Low Risk.
